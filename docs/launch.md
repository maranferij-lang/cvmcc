## Post for DOU (forum)

**I built a tool that reads your CV like a recruiter and honestly tells you what to cut**

I had a dream job and a CV, but no plan for getting from one to the other. I sat down with AI and went through my CV line by line: what a recruiter in this field looks for, what to cut, what to learn and how to write it down. GetCVmax came out of that.

What it does:
1. Gives a score out of 100 on 7 criteria for your field.
2. Marks every line: keep, cut, rewrite or move, and explains why.
3. Rewrites for a specific job posting. It does not invent numbers: where there is no number, it puts [N] and asks you.
4. Asks up to 8 questions that pull out forgotten results.
5. Tells you what to build up for your dream job: courses, hackathons, certificates with deadlines.
6. Returns a ready one-page CV in PDF and DOCX.

Why not ChatGPT: it happily adds numbers that never existed, and you will have to defend them in the interview.

A recruiter looks at a CV for 7.4 seconds on average (Ladders eye-tracking study, 2018). In that time the CV must say the main thing.

## Faceless slideshow (TikTok, Reels, Shorts)

Format: 5 slides, white background, black text, red pen. The CV lines are in English, because the CV is in English. The last slide is always: "Check your CV: getcvmax.com". All lines are made up, we do not show real CVs.

1. **"Responsible for"**
   1) This line is in half of student CVs. 2) ~~Responsible for making reports in Excel~~ 3) Built [N] weekly Excel reports on sales for the regional team 4) A recruiter cares what you did and for whom, not what you were responsible for.
2. **Date of birth**
   1) Applying to London? Remove this. 2) ~~Date of birth: 01.01.2004~~ 3) In the UK and US, age is not written in a CV. 4) Use the freed line for a result.
3. **Student council**
   1) "Member of" says nothing. 2) ~~Member of Student Council~~ 3) Organised 6 university events for 300+ students 4) The numbers were in your head, not in the CV.
4. **Hard-working team player**
   1) Everyone writes these words. 2) ~~Hard-working team player with strong communication skills~~ 3) Cut it. Prove it with an experience line where a team achieved something together with you.
5. **MS Office**
   1) Nobody hires you for Word. 2) ~~Proficient in MS Office~~ 3) Excel (pivot tables, XLOOKUP), SQL (joins, GROUP BY) 4) Write the tools that are in the job posting.
6. **Fluent English**
   1) "Fluent" cannot be verified. 2) ~~Fluent English~~ 3) English C1 (IELTS 7.5) 4) A level and a certificate instead of an adjective.
7. **Photo**
   1) A photo in a CV for the US? 2) ~~[photo]~~ 3) In the US and UK photos are removed to avoid bias in selection. 4) The space is better used for a project.
8. **Two pages**
   1) An intern with a 2-page CV. 2) The recruiter will finish the first page. 3) Cut everything that is not about this job. 4) One page, the strongest on top.
9. **Objective**
   1) Nobody reads this paragraph. 2) ~~Objective: to obtain a challenging position in a dynamic company~~ 3) Data analyst intern · SQL, Python, Tableau 4) One line: who you are and what you can do.
10. **ChatGPT invented numbers**
   1) I asked ChatGPT to improve my CV. 2) "Increased sales by 300%" 3) You did not increase sales. In the interview they will ask how. 4) We put [N] and ask you for the real number.
