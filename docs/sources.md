recruiters and practical guides. The example items in the rubrics are made up and serve only as a style sample.

## Regional conventions and screening order
Files `cvmax/rubrics/regions/*.md` and the "Screening order" sections of the program rubrics. Each source
below was opened and checked against the claim it supports (October 2026). Sources marked "via search"
refused direct access and were checked through search results from the same publisher.

### US / Canada
- Penn Career Services, Write a Resume/CV: https://careerservices.upenn.edu/channels/resume/ (one page for undergraduates; City, State/Country instead of a street address; PDF named with your name; ATS cannot read tables or graphics)
- UC Berkeley Career Engagement, Resumes: https://career.berkeley.edu/prepare-for-success/resumes/ (one page for students; a CV is an academic document; GPA if 3.0 or above; no headers, footers, tables or graphics; follow the posting's file format)
- UConn Center for Career Readiness, Writing a compelling U.S.-style resume (Oct 2025): https://career.uconn.edu/blog/2025/10/14/writing-a-compelling-u-s-style-resume/ (omit photo, marital status, religion, gender identity, age, immigration status; keep the original grade and add a US-scale equivalent)
- Ohio State ASC Career Success, Specialized resume guidance: https://asccareersuccess.osu.edu/specialized-resume-guidance-population-and-industry (convert an international GPA to the 4.0 scale with WES iGPA or Scholaro; work authorization need not be on the resume)
- Pomona College CDO, U.S. resume tips for international students: https://cdo.pomona.edu/resources/u-s-resume-tips-for-international-students/ (no photo, age, marital status, religion or visa status)
- U.S. EEOC, Prohibited employment policies/practices: https://www.eeoc.gov/prohibited-employment-policiespractices (employers should not ask for a photograph of an applicant)
- U.S. FTC, Employer background checks and your rights: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/employer-background-checks-and-your-rights (no Social Security number on a resume)
- Government of Canada Job Bank, Write a good resume (2024-05-30): https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/findajob/resources/write-good-resume (limit to two pages; a photo is not the norm in Canada; leave out age, marital status, religion; never the SIN)
- UTM Career Centre, Resume checklist (Jul 2024): https://utm.utoronto.ca/careers/sites/files/careers/documents/2024-07/Resume%20Checklist.pdf (one or two pages; formatting that works with ATS)
- CIHR, English style guide: https://cihr-irsc.gc.ca/e/44167.html (Canadian spelling: -our, -re, -ize, e.g. behaviour, centre, analyze)
- Yale Office of Career Strategy, Resume to CV conversion: https://ocs.yale.edu/resources/resume-to-cv-conversion/ (most US employers ask for a resume; CVs for academic posts)
- WES, Credential evaluations for Ukrainian students (2017): https://www.wes.org/advisor-blog/wes-credential-evaluations-for-ukrainian-students/ (Dyplom Bakalavra = bachelor's, Dyplom Magistra = master's; iGPA calculator for GPA)
- WES, How to market your international education to employers (Aug 2024): https://www.wes.org/how-to-market-your-international-education-to-employers/ (list the degree equivalency once evaluated)
- NACE, Employer use of skills-based hiring grows (Jan 2026): https://www.naceweb.org/job-market/trends-and-predictions/employer-use-of-skills-based-hiring-practices-grows (42% of employers screen by GPA, down from 73% in 2019)
- NACE, Internship experience often the deciding factor (Feb 2026): https://naceweb.org/talent-acquisition/candidate-selection/internship-experience-often-the-deciding-factor-between-equal-candidates (internships with the organisation or industry are the only attributes with "very much influence")
- NACE, High-impact skills students should showcase (Apr 2026): https://naceweb.org/about-us/press/2026/the-high-impact-skills-college-students-should-showcase-on-their-resumes (employers want examples, not lists of skills)
- University of Pittsburgh Career Central, ATS tips (Feb 2025): https://careercentral.pitt.edu/blog/2025/02/12/resume-and-cv-writing-tips-applicant-tracking-systems-ats/ (most companies rely on ATS; nothing in headers or footers; avoid columns)

- University of Birmingham, Birmingham Business School Careers: https://intranet.birmingham.ac.uk/student/careers/bbs-careers/cvs-cover-letters-and-application-support.aspx (two pages standard; one page for investment banks and consulting; remove graphics and tables for uploads)
