# Learning resources: editorial review

This is the list of every learning resource GetCVmax shows to users, with the reason each one is there and the evidence a person saw. It follows the seven principles in `docs/plan-resources-and-free-launch.md`, Part E. Researchers proposed the entries, independent verifiers opened every page and evidence link on 2026-10-11, and a final editorial pass (this document) removed weak, duplicate and paid-without-reason entries and fixed wording that claimed more than the evidence shows.

Shipped: 114 resources (102 free or free to audit, 12 in Ukrainian). Held back: 26 (see "Held back and why").

**How to veto an entry.** Delete its object (find the id shown under the title) from `cvmax/resources/catalog/<cluster>.json`, or tell the assistant the id and the reason. If you want to keep a record, move it to `cvmax/resources/held_back.json` with a `held_back_reason`. The tests in `tests/test_resources_catalog.py` keep the rules: at most 3 resources and at most 1 paid resource per skill (counted by the first skill tag), no duplicate ids or links, and every entry valid and checked.

Reading the tables: the skill column lists all tags, the first is the main one. The app shows an entry under every skill it is tagged with. Hours marked (est.) are our estimate; the others come from the provider's page. In the cost column, paid and freemium entries show the price and what it covers (the `price_note` field). "Verified" is the date a person opened the page and how (fetched means the page itself was read).

## What we could not verify

Facts in the catalog that rest on inference, a single source, or a page that can change without notice.

- **"Free" inferred from a platform-wide statement, not the course page.** Diia.Osvita courses (`diia-osvita-*`, `diia-dpo-training-course-uk`): the FAQ says the series are free; the DPO page itself shows no price. Microsoft Learn (`microsoft-learn-prepare-and-visualize-data-with-power-bi`): free per the Learn FAQ; the hosted lab VM is described, not priced. AWS Cloud Practitioner Essentials: the course runs on Skill Builder, which needs JavaScript and was not opened; "free" rests on AWS's training page and a 2020 blog updated in 2023. The Diia interview simulator: "free" rests on the Ministry's statements in the press.
- **Free access that the provider says can change.** `deeplearning-ai-chatgpt-prompt-engineering-for-developers` is free "for a limited time during the platform beta".
- **Prices.** Coursera prices are the US Coursera Plus price ($59 a month, $399 a year); prices differ by country and a single course can also be bought separately ($49 to $79 a month). The Google certificate price ($49 a month) is stated for the US and Canada only. The TOEFL fee is the Ukraine price from ETS's fee data file and excludes taxes. AWS Amplify costs are AWS's "typically" figures. The Google Analytics certification price was not seen. Every paid or freemium entry carries a `price_note` that says what the price covers ("per month with Coursera Plus", "per exam attempt"); the app shows it next to the price and the cost column below repeats it. The AWS static-website tutorial is "freemium" only in the sense that AWS usage is about $1 to $3 a month after the free tier; there is no separate full version.
