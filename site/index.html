questions

Before you start

Can I try it for free?

Yes. GetCVmax is free while we launch. Each person gets up to 2 CV reviews, 2 Q&A interviews and 3 PDF exports a day. Limits reset daily, and there is also a daily cap for the whole site, so on a very busy day you may be asked to come back tomorrow.

Will it make things up?

No. It only rewrites what's in your CV or what you tell it in the interview. Where a number is missing, it leaves a gap like N and asks you to fill it.

Is my CV used to train AI?

No. Your CV goes to Anthropic's Claude API. Under Anthropic's commercial terms it may not train its models on what we send, and it deletes inputs and outputs from its systems within 30 days (longer only if its safety systems flag a policy violation). You can delete your results from your account at any time.

Which CVs does it work with?

English CVs in PDF, for internships and first jobs in business, finance, consulting, tech, data, marketing, law and more. Each field has its own checklist.

How long does it take?

About 2 minutes for the review and 5 to 10 for the interview and download.

Is there a subscription?

No. There is nothing to pay and nothing to cancel. If paid options come later, we will email you before anything changes, and everything you made stays yours.

How do you pick learning resources?

A person checks every resource and records what they saw and when. Free comes first: a paid one appears only when it is clearly better than the free options, with its price. There are no sponsors and no affiliate links. Each one needs a track record, such as a recognised institution or vendor, public ratings with a real number of reviews, or a recommendation from a university or government career service. Hands-on beats video-only, so every plan ends with something you can put on your CV. If we have no vetted resource for a skill, we say so instead of guessing. Links are re-checked every week and broken ones are hidden.
