Before you pay

Can I try it for free?

No, but the sample at the top of this page is a real review format: the score, line verdicts and rewrites you get. If the review gives you nothing useful, you get your money back within 14 days.

Will it make things up?

No. It only rewrites what's in your CV or what you tell it in the interview. Where a number is missing, it leaves a gap like N and asks you to fill it.

Is my CV used to train AI?

No. We use Google's paid Gemini API, which doesn't train on your data. You can delete your results from your account at any time.

Which CVs does it work with?

English CVs in PDF, for internships and first jobs in business, finance, consulting, tech, data, marketing, law and more. Each field has its own checklist.

About 2 minutes for the review and 5 to 10 for the interview and download.
