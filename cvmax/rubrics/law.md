# Program rubric: Law (KSE)

Typical targets: law firm intern/junior associate, in-house legal in tech or banks, compliance,
policy analyst, international organisations, legal tech.

Signals employers look for:
- Areas of law named precisely (corporate/M&A, IP, tax, sanctions, GDPR/data protection, international
  humanitarian law, arbitration).
- Moot courts and competitions with placement and role (oralist, researcher).
- Legal writing: memos, research, publications, legal clinic cases (number, type).
- Languages: English legal level, certificates (e.g. TOLES, ILEC) are a plus for international firms.
- For compliance/legal tech: understanding of regulation plus tools or data skills.

Typical mistakes:
- Listing courses of the curriculum instead of practical legal work.
- Vague "legal research" with no area or output.
- Local conventions (photo, personal data) on CVs for international firms.
