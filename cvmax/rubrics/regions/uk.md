## Regional conventions: UK
- Length: two full A4 pages is standard; one for investment banking, consulting or thin experience. Never 1.5 pages.
- Cut photo, date of birth, marital status, nationality: they reveal Equality Act 2010 protected characteristics. Add a visa line instead: "Right to work: Graduate visa to Aug 2027, no sponsorship needed until then".
- Header: name, phone, email, LinkedIn, city. Home address optional; never on a CV posted online.
- Dates in month words: "Sep 2023 - Jun 2027", not numeric. Numbers: "£12,000", "4.6/5", "85%".
- UK spelling throughout: organise, modelling, programme, analysed. Call it a CV; "resume" is the US term.
- Education: degree, university, dates, class ("Predicted: 2:1"). Foreign degree: keep the original grade and its maximum; no self-conversion. UK ENIC compares the level. University tables differ (Ukrainian 2:1: 80/100 or 4.0-4.5/5), so any UK class is approximate.
- Screened: grades against a stated minimum (47% of ISE employers require a 2:1), internships, spring/insight weeks, placements (77% rate ex-interns more work-ready), advert keywords.
- ATS: large employers screen by ATS or an online form instead of a CV. Send the format the advert names; if none, .docx (PDF when a person reads it). Single column; no tables, headers, footers or graphics.
- Unlike the general rubric: accept two full pages; US spelling and numeric dates are Language quality errors; flag a missing degree class or a self-converted one.
