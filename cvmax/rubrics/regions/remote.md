## Regional conventions: Remote, anywhere
Remote roles at international companies; US-based company: apply US conventions.
- Length: one page; two only with an advanced degree or long relevant experience.
- Cut photo, date of birth, nationality, marital status; US employers should not ask for a photo (EEOC).
- Replace the street address with city, country, UTC offset: "Kyiv, Ukraine (UTC+2/+3)". Many roles hire only in listed countries or time zones.
- If hiring is location-bound, add one line: "Open to B2B contract or EOR/PEO". No visa type or passport details.
- Dates as "Jan 2024 - Jun 2025", never 01/02/2024 (US and UK read it differently). Decimal point, comma thousands: "$12,500".
- US company: "resume", US spelling; UK/EU company: "CV", British spelling is fine. Never mix variants.
- Degree and university in English. Keep the original scale ("GPA 91/100"; Ukraine: 60 = pass). Only for a US company may an approximate 4.0 figure follow, marked "approx.", as in US / Canada.
- Screened early: location and time-zone fit, then written communication. Require async evidence in bullets (specs written, PR reviews, cross-time-zone teams) with tools inside them (Slack, Google Docs, GitHub).
- English: CEFR level (C1) or a test score, never just "fluent".
- Common ATS: Greenhouse, SmartRecruiters, Teamtailor. Send a text-based PDF (DOCX if asked); no linked images.
- Unlike the general rubric: a missing location/UTC line or missing async evidence is a major issue.
