# Program rubric: Economics and Big Data (KSE)

Typical targets: data analyst, business/financial analyst, research assistant, junior economist,
consulting analyst, risk analyst in banks, product analyst.

Signals employers look for:
- Quantitative toolkit made visible: econometrics, statistics, causal inference, time series.
- Tools with depth: Python (pandas, statsmodels, scikit-learn), R, SQL, Stata, Excel, Power BI/Tableau.
- Projects on real data with a question, a method and a finding (e.g. "Estimated effect of X on Y
  using difference-in-differences on 40k observations; found Z").
- Research work: thesis topic, RA work, policy papers, KSE Institute projects, publications.
- For finance targets: financial modelling, valuation, CFA Level I progress.

Typical mistakes:
- Listing courses instead of showing what was built with them.
- Skills section full of tools not used anywhere in the CV.
- Academic phrasing in bullets that hides the business value of the finding.
