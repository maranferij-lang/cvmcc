# Program rubric: Psychology (KSE)

Typical targets: HR/recruitment, people analytics, UX research, research assistant, mental health
and psychosocial support programs in NGOs, L&D, customer research, product/market research.

Signals employers look for:
- Research skills made concrete: study design, surveys, interviews, sample sizes, statistics in
  SPSS/R/JASP/Python, findings.
- For UX research: interviews and usability tests run, insights that changed a product, Figma basics.
- For HR: recruitment funnels, number of candidates screened, onboarding programs, HR tools.
- For NGO/psychosocial roles: trainings completed (e.g. PFA), supervised practice hours, target groups.
- Clear separation between academic work, practice and volunteering.

Typical mistakes:
- Theory-heavy descriptions instead of what was done and for whom.
- No numbers (participants, sessions, respondents).
- Missing the bridge between psychology and the business role targeted.
